Fernbrook Systems wins Small Business IT Award

By Regional Staff · Midland Commerce Report

Fernbrook Systems, the managed-IT firm that has spent the better part of a decade quietly keeping local offices online, was named this year's recipient of the Small Business IT Excellence Award at the regional Chamber of Commerce banquet.

The award, presented annually to a company judged to have made an outsized contribution to the technical reliability of the region's small businesses, recognized Fernbrook's work supporting medical offices, contractors, and nonprofits across the county with helpdesk, network, and security services.

"We started this company out of a garage answering support calls one at a time. Getting recognized for that same kind of work a decade later is honestly a little surreal."

Support Lead Marcus Ilyin accepted the award on behalf of the company, crediting the team's habit of "showing up when we say we will" as the difference between Fernbrook and larger, less responsive providers. He noted that the company has grown from two people to eleven since opening in 2015, while keeping its client-first approach largely unchanged.
